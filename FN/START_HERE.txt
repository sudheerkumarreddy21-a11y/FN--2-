STOCK FILTER - P/B SCREENER (v2)

1. Open the stock-filter- folder and read README.md.
2. Double-click RUN_PROJECT.bat (needs Node.js). Fill in .env on the first run, then run it again.
   For a quick test without SMS, set OTP_DEV_MODE=true in .env - the OTP appears in the terminal.
3. Open http://localhost:8888, create an account, verify the OTP, and click any stock for its chart and BUY/HOLD/SELL signal.
